# Invoice & Billing Management System

A web-based application for creating, managing, and tracking customer invoices and billing records.

##  Project Status

In Development

##  Overview

The Invoice & Billing Management System is designed to simplify billing operations by allowing businesses to create invoices, calculate totals, track payments, and maintain invoice records.

##  Planned Features

- Customer management
- Product and service management
- Invoice creation
- Automatic subtotal calculation
- Discount calculation
- Tax calculation
- Invoice status tracking
- Paid and pending invoices
- Payment records
- Invoice history
- Sales summary dashboard

##  Technology Stack
